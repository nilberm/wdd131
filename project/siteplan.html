Maple Routes

Site Plan for the Canada Travel Guide

1. Site Name

Maple Routes

The name joins the maple leaf, the best known symbol of Canada, with routes, the main thing the site offers: suggested road trips and itineraries across the provinces. It is short, easy to remember, and clearly tells visitors that the site is about traveling through Canada.

Optional domain availability: mapleroutes.ca

2. Site Purpose

Maple Routes is an informational travel guide that helps visitors plan a trip across Canada. The site provides:

  • Points of interest organized by province
  • Suggested road trip routes with several stops
  • Practical travel tips such as seasons, driving, and budgeting
  • A trip planner form where visitors can save preferred destinations and travel dates

3. Scenarios

These questions come from the target audience, travelers who are planning a first or return visit to Canada.

  1. What are the best places to visit in Ontario if I only have one week?
  2. Which road trip route should I take to see the Rocky Mountains?
  3. When is the best season to visit Quebec for a first trip?
  4. How can I save my favorite destinations and travel dates to plan later?
